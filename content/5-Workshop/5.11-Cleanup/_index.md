Delete the endpoint first, wait for deletion, then remove the endpoint configuration, newly deployed API, and Lambda. Inspect capture data before deleting it. Keep raw/model artifacts, Pipeline, and Registry evidence unless they are no longer needed. Historical cleanup is recorded in `report/cleanup_report.json`.
